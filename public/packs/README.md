# Word packs

Optional downloads for YOUrigin's dictionary and thesaurus. Built by
`scripts/build-word-packs.mjs`; not part of the app's offline cache until
someone downloads one in Settings.

## en-wordnet.json.gz

Definitions and synonyms from **Open English WordNet** (2025 edition),
https://en-word.net, © the Open English WordNet team and Princeton University.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Converted to a compact format;
no wording was changed.

## en-moby.json.gz

Related words from **Moby Thesaurus II** by Grady Ward, placed in the public
domain (Project Gutenberg eBook #3202).
